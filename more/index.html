MORE

Less phone handling. More of the morning you enjoy. Morning Operator learns from what you choose to share, proposes one routine, and follows it once you approve.

PlatformNative iPhone app, iOS 26
ModelsOn the device, Apple models
AccountsNone: no MORE account, no backend

The idea

Most mornings start by picking up the phone and losing twenty minutes to it.

MORE turns the evidence you explicitly share about your mornings into one routine you review and approve, then walks you through it: a short spoken opening with the weather and your calendar, your mail, one article, one activity you chose, and a recap at the end. Nothing runs until you approve it, and the learning happens on the phone.

A morning, step by step

  1. Choose and learnPick the sources it may learn from: Screen Time, a screen recording you make or import, and your apps.
  2. Review and editIt proposes a routine and cites the dated mornings it came from. Keep, drop or resize each moment.
  3. Approve onceApproving saves the routine and starts nothing. The same routine is reused every day.
  4. Start a morningA concise opening with weather and calendar, then unread Gmail, read inside the app.
  5. One activityOpen the activity you chose with a timer, and +2 or Next from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
  6. Finish and reviewA recap of what you did, skipped or stopped, saved on the phone, with room for your thoughts.

Architecture

Eight modules in one Swift app with a widget: experience shell, connections, evidence and learning, the approved plan, the morning run, briefing, review and privacy, and delivery. Raw recordings stay local, and there is no silent remote fallback.

Privacy by design

What it does

  • Learns only from what you explicitly share, and shows which dates it used
  • Runs extraction and the model on the phone
  • Lets you forget everything it learned

What it never does

  • Send mail or create remote drafts
  • Start a morning, or change the routine, without you
  • Claim a habit the evidence does not show: a missing day is not a zero
