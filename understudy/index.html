Understudy

Watch a person do a task once. Ask them what the camera could not see. Rehearse it on a simulated robot, on Earth and in orbit, and fix what fails.

Open the live demo Watch the video (2:57) Source
Built forNebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon, Physical AI track
ModelsNVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory
RobotSO-101 arm, simulated in MuJoCo

Why

Teaching a robot is expensive, and nobody asks the person who did the task what mattered.

On a space station every crew hour is scarce: NASA prices one hour of ISS crew time at $130,000 for commercial activities. The robots meant to take over routine crew work need a clear task, a rehearsed program and labelled data. NASA already films crew procedures all the time. Understudy turns one such clip into the first two, and rehearses the result in orbit, where a released object drifts instead of resting. An understudy learns a role by watching the lead, then rehearses it on stage. This does that for robots.

How it works

  1. WatchThe clip stays in the browser. Only a 480 px, 4 fps proxy goes to the model. For a gesture, MediaPipe traces the hand.
  2. LabelA vision model turns the proxy into a task spec: timed steps, objects with size and symmetry, and targets.
  3. AskA second model writes the robot program. Every number it would have to guess becomes a question for the person.
  4. RehearseA simulated SO-101 arm runs the answered program in the browser, under Earth gravity and at 0 g.
  5. RepairWhen a rehearsal fails, the simulator's note goes back to the model, which patches the program, up to five times.
  6. ExportThe passing rollouts download as a LeRobot v2.1 skeleton, with a quality report that says what it is and is not.

Architecture

The routes are stateless and replayable: without a key, or when Token Factory stops listing a model, every step answers from its recording and says why.

What it proves, and what it does not

Proves

  • The clarified task can be executed by a scripted SO-101 program, on Earth and in orbit
  • Its success criteria can be computed by a simulator
  • A model can repair a failing program from text notes, cheaply

Does not prove

  • That a learned policy would succeed, or that a real grasp holds
  • Orbit is rigid-body physics at 0 g: no air currents, tethers or flight rules
  • The export is format-valid, not training data
